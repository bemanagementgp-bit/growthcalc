Privacy Policy

Last updated: . This policy explains what information GrowthCalc (“we”, “us”) does and does not collect when you use this website.

Your calculations stay on your device

Every calculation – your initial investment, contributions, rates, scenarios and goals – is performed entirely in your browser with JavaScript. The numbers you enter are never sent to our servers. To make the tool convenient on return visits, your inputs, saved scenarios, table view, theme preference and Pro licence status are stored in your browser's localStorage. You can clear this at any time with the “Reset” button or by clearing your browser's site data.

If you use the “Share link” button, your inputs are encoded into the page URL you copy. Anyone you send that link to can see those inputs; the link is not stored by us.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense to keep the calculator free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before such cookies are set. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (page views, approximate location, device type and interactions such as “copy” or “share”). This data is aggregated and does not include the values you type into the calculator. Google Analytics may set cookies; you can block them with a browser extension such as the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages may contain links to brokers or financial products marked as partner links. If you click one and open an account or make a purchase we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. These links are clearly identified and do not influence the calculator's results. Partner links are not a recommendation to invest in any product.

Pro purchases

Pro upgrades are processed by a third-party payment provider (such as Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad or Stripe). We never see or store your card details. The provider's own privacy policy applies to the checkout. Your licence key is stored only in your browser's localStorage.

Cookies summary

  • Essential (localStorage): theme, inputs, scenarios, licence status – no tracking.
  • Advertising cookies: set by Google AdSense only if ads are enabled.
  • Analytics cookies: set by Google Analytics only if analytics is enabled.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version is always available at this address. Questions can be sent through the contact details published on the main site or the payment provider's receipt.
